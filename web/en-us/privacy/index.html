Privacy Policy

Effective 4 August 2026 · Last updated 4 August 2026

Written is an iPhone dating app. It builds your profile out of what you already listen to, watch, plan and do, so that you do not have to write one. That only works if you connect accounts to it, which means this document has to be specific rather than reassuring.

This policy covers the Written iPhone app, its share extension, and this website. It is written in the first person plural — "we" is the team that makes Written.

What we collect

What you tell us

  • Your account. You sign in with Apple. We receive the identifier Apple gives us and, if you allow it, your name and email address. We never see your Apple password.
  • Your profile. First name, date of birth, gender, the district you live in, and optionally education and occupation.
  • Your photographs. Up to six, in the order you arranged them.
  • How you want to be approached. Two answers, given during sign-up, about flirtatiousness and how quickly you reply.
  • What you write. Messages you send, people you like or decline, links you share into the app, and anything you report.

What your connected accounts give us

Nothing is connected unless you tap Connect and grant it. Each source can be disconnected again in the app.

  • YouTube — read-only. Your subscriptions, liked videos, playlists and the contents of those playlists. We do not request, and cannot obtain, your watch history.
  • Apple Music — your library, playlists, recently added and recently played music, heavy rotation, recommendations, and the tracks you have marked liked or disliked.
  • Apple Podcasts — the episodes held on your device and the shows they belong to.
  • Apple Calendar — event titles, dates, locations, organisers and any booking link, within the window the app reads. Birthday and public-holiday calendars are excluded.
  • Apple Health — workouts, exercise minutes, active energy, steps, walking and running distance, and your date of birth and biological sex.

Health data never leaves your phone. It is read on the device, reduced there to a small number of general signals — roughly when you are awake, which sports you do and at what level, and an average daily step count — and the underlying workouts and samples are then discarded without ever being uploaded. Only those signals are stored on our servers.

What we work out

From the above we derive the readings that make a profile: the subjects you care about, and where your interests, preferences and temperament sit relative to other people's. Some of these derived subjects — an artist, a channel, a kind of music — appear on the card other people see.

Why we collect it

  • To build and show your profile, which is the product.
  • To order what each person is shown about you by what the two of you have in common.
  • To let you like, match with and message other people.
  • To keep the service working, and to investigate reports of abuse.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with data brokers. Nobody on our team reads your messages or your distillation except where you have asked us to look at something or where we are investigating a report.

What other people can see

Two things are visible to other signed-in users of Written, and nothing else is:

  • Your card — first name, age, district, your photographs, and derived subjects such as an artist or a channel name. It deliberately contains nothing from which a distillation could be reconstructed.
  • Anything you choose to share — a link you publish with a sentence about it, shown with your first name.

Your conversations are visible only to you and the person you are talking to. Nothing in Written is public on the open internet.

YouTube

Written uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The scope we request is youtube.readonly and nothing else.

We hold YouTube titles, channel names and playlist contents for no more than 30 calendar days. After that they are deleted or refreshed from YouTube; what remains is the derived reading, which names nothing.

You can disconnect YouTube in the app at any time, and we will delete the data obtained from it within 7 days. You can also revoke our access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions; where you revoke it there, we delete within 30 days.

Where it is kept, and who else touches it

Your data is stored in a Postgres database and file storage operated by Supabase, on servers in the United States. Access is enforced row by row: a signed-in account can read its own rows, and the two tables described under What other people can see, and nothing else.

One other service is contacted, and only with two words: to show the lyrics of a song, we send that song's artist and title — no account identifier, nothing about you — to lrclib.net, and to music.163.com if the first has no answer.

Apple provides sign-in and the app's distribution. This website is served by Cloudflare.

How long we keep it

  • YouTube-derived text — 30 calendar days, as above.
  • Everything else — for as long as your account exists.
  • After you delete your account — removed. Ordinary backups may retain copies for a short period before they roll over.

What you can do

  • Disconnect a source at any time in the app.
  • Correct your profile from the Memories tab. Every field there can be changed after it is first set.
  • Delete your account from the Memories tab. This removes your profile, your distillation, your photographs and your conversations.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold, or ask us to delete something specific, by writing to the address below.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights — to object to processing, to restrict it, or to complain to a supervisory authority. Write to us and we will honour them.

Children

Written is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly keep data about anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top, and where the change is significant we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
